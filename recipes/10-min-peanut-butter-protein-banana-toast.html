10-Min Peanut Butter Protein Banana Toast

(35g Protein) - Busy Morning Essential

Ingredients

2 slices whole grain bread
4 tbsp powdered peanut butter (or regular)
1 scoop whey protein powder
1 small banana, sliced
100g plain Greek yogurt
1 tsp chia seeds
Cinnamon sprinkle

Instructions

1

Toast the bread slices until golden.

2

Mix peanut butter powder with water to make paste, stir in protein powder.

3

Spread on toast, top with banana slices and Greek yogurt dollops.

4

Sprinkle chia seeds and cinnamon. Enjoy immediately!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use regular peanut butter?

Yes, but use powdered PB to keep calories low while boosting protein. Regular PB adds healthy fats but more calories.

Is this recipe gluten-free?

Use gluten-free bread. All other ingredients are naturally gluten-free. Check protein powder label.
